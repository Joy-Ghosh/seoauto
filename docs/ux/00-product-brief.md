# Product brief: finish a worthwhile SEO fix

Status: proposed product foundation, 27 November 2026. The audience order is the owner's decision. Needs, willingness to pay and usability predictions remain hypotheses until research.

## Product decision

SEO Workbench helps freelance SEOs and vibe coders turn website evidence into a reviewed change and confirm whether the published page changed. The unit of value is a completed, verified improvement—not a scan, generated report, decorative score or number of AI calls.

Positioning draft: **Know what to fix. Understand why. Check the result.**

Supporting copy: “Find worthwhile SEO changes, review the page evidence, and keep each fix moving from draft to verification.” This describes the intended product, not a claim of increased traffic.

Today the application is a local/private audit and planning workspace with optional research and measurement tools. It is not yet a public customer-account service or a universal live website editor. See [current evidence](01-current-product-evidence.md).

## Who comes first

1. Freelance SEO practitioners: evaluate a site, prioritise defensible work, explain it to a client or implementer, check delivery.
2. Vibe coders: understand a finding, get an exact reviewable change, apply it in their code/editor/CMS, confirm the result without guessing.
3. Business owners using WordPress, Shopify or another website platform: need plain-language decisions and a safe implementation handoff. Platform detection alone does not grant editing access.
4. Agencies: require multi-client ownership, collaboration, dependable records and stronger proof. Support this after the single-site loop works; do not pretend a website dropdown is agency project management.

The first two audiences share an outcome but differ in vocabulary and handoff. Offer optional “Explain this” and “Implementation details” within the same task, rather than forcing a persona questionnaire before scanning.

## Job and alternatives

Working hypothesis: “When I have limited time to improve a website, help me choose a useful, supported change, understand exactly what to edit, and prove what happened, so I can deliver confidently.”

Likely existing alternatives to investigate: browser inspection, spreadsheet checklists, general AI chat, a crawler report, Search Console and a website editor. Their actual use and pain have not been interviewed. We have no measured customer acquisition, retention, revenue, market-size or product-market-fit evidence.

Why now is a hypothesis: AI-assisted site creation can make execution faster while leaving quality evaluation difficult. Ask participants for their most recent real SEO change instead of assuming they share this pain.

## MVP: one complete loop

1. Add a website and understand data/storage scope.
2. Scan a bounded set of publicly reachable HTML pages, with cancellation and honest coverage.
3. Review a short, explainable queue. Each item shows a specific observed problem, affected page and reason to act.
4. Inspect evidence. Write a replacement manually or explicitly request an AI draft.
5. Save current and proposed values in a plan. Support title and description first; alt-text editing requires meaningful image context.
6. Copy/export the exact change and implementation instructions. User applies it in the website editor; do not mark it published automatically.
7. Recheck the page. Record expected versus observed values, scan times and checks not covered.
8. Return to the remaining plan without losing context.

Existing pieces support much of this. The MVP work is joining the pieces into a reliable task, strengthening persistence and replacing ambiguous completion states. H1, structured-data and link suggestions can remain clearly labelled advice until typed plan items and verification exist for them. Do not imply that the metadata plan already persists or applies those changes.

AI, GSC, PageSpeed, competitor comparison and bulk titles are optional enrichment. None should gate the first useful manual fix. A deep audit stays available after the recommended queue.

## Anti-goals for this MVP

- Universal automatic publishing, ranking guarantees, paid keyword/backlink databases, invented competitor metrics.
- Multi-client collaboration, public unlimited crawling or an app-owner-funded public AI service.
- A single “SEO health” score that mixes rules, heuristics and search performance.
- Replacing a user's CMS, code editor or judgement.
- A mascot, animation or onboarding tour that delays evidence.

The eventual ten-star vision is a reviewable change workflow across supported CMS/repositories, with preview, permissions, conflict detection, publish, rollback and independent live verification. That is a later product capability, not a slogan for today's implementation.

## How people should feel

| Moment | Intended feeling | How the product earns it |
|---|---|---|
| First scan | Oriented | One website field, scope explanation, optional sample |
| Waiting | In control | Real counts/current phase, cancel, preserved work |
| Findings | Curious, not accused | Plain reasons, affected pages, small next step |
| AI draft | Helped, not overruled | Editable output, sources, unknowns, original visible |
| Applying | Prepared | Exact destination/value, copy/export, publishing boundary |
| Recheck | Confident about what was checked | Expected/actual, timestamp, coverage and failures |
| Return visit | Able to resume | Persistent plan, latest activity, next unfinished task |

“Fun” means manageable work, satisfying closure and a recognisable visual voice. It does not mean gamifying anxiety or celebrating unverified progress.

## Success and decision gates

Proposed primary metric: percentage of consenting pilot sessions that complete a supported change and get an observed-value match, with implementation destination recorded. Do not equate it with traffic improvement or attribute all user edits to this product.

Instrument locally or in an opt-in pilot: scan started/completed/partial, first evidence opened, draft saved, implementation handoff used, recheck requested/result. Never log API keys, passwords, full page text or client URLs to third parties by default. Current baselines are unknown.

Provisional first-round gates, not observed results: participants can explain the main finding and identify the next action without moderator help; all participants distinguish saved, applied and verified; no unreadable/unscanned item appears verified. Record time to the first decision after a scan finishes and set numerical targets after the baseline round; external scan/publishing duration is recorded separately. Small samples are directional, not market validation.

Go: document and prototype the verified-fix loop, then test with the confirmed priority audiences. No-go for a broad public/agency launch until we have repeated useful sessions, reliable storage/recovery and credible verification. The next phase is [the screen/action contract](05-information-architecture-and-copy.md), not another unstructured visual pass.
